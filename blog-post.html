How to get great marketing copy from AI

5 min read

AI can write a decent post in seconds. Getting a great one takes a little direction. These four habits make the biggest difference.

1. Start with the outcome, not the format

“Write an Instagram post” gives you something generic. “Write an Instagram post that gets new runners to sign up for our Saturday 5K” gives you something that works. Say who it's for and what you want them to do.

2. Give one concrete detail

A price, a deadline, an ingredient, a customer problem. One specific fact makes copy feel real instead of salesy.

Weak: “Try our amazing coffee.” Strong: “18 hours steeped, zero bitterness — your first can is $1.”

3. Pick the tone on purpose

A luxury brand and a student café shouldn't sound the same. Choose the tone that matches your audience, then generate a couple of versions and keep the best lines.

  • Playful for social media and younger audiences
  • Professional for B2B and LinkedIn
  • Minimal for premium products

4. Keep a human in the loop

AI is a brilliant first draft and a tireless brainstorming partner. Read the result, adjust a word or two to sound like you, and check facts like prices and dates before you publish.
