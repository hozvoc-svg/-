Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy explains what information Muse collects, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Your account details (name, email and, if you use Google sign-in, your Google profile basics), the prompts you write and the content Muse generates for you, and basic technical data such as your IP address, which we use to prevent abuse.

How we use information

To provide the service, save your history, manage your plan and credits, prevent abuse and answer support requests. We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

We rely on trusted providers to run Muse: Supabase (accounts and database), Anthropic (the Claude AI model that writes your content), Lemon Squeezy (payments and billing, acting as merchant of record) and Vercel (hosting). They process data only to provide their services to us.

AI and your content

Your prompts are sent to Anthropic’s Claude API to generate your content. Your content is not used to train AI models.

Payments

Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy. We never see or store your full card details.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. To delete your account and data, email us and we will remove it within 30 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, and to object to certain processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

Contact

Questions? Email us at support@example.com.
