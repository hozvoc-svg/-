About us

Everyone deserves a world-class creative team

We started Muse because great content shouldn't depend on big budgets. Today 120,000+ creators and teams use it to bring their ideas to life.

Our story

From a side project to 85 million creations

Muse began in 2023 as a weekend tool for writing product descriptions. Friends asked for images, then video, then team features. Two years later, Muse powers marketing for startups, agencies and solo creators in more than 140 countries.

We're a remote team of 38 designers, engineers and storytellers who believe AI should amplify human creativity — not replace it.
