You're probably scrolling right now. Here's why you can't stop.
Every swipe is like a slot machine. You never know if the next video will be boring or amazing.
That uncertainty is what makes your brain release dopamine. Not the reward itself. The anticipation.
And the feed never ends. There's no last page, so your brain never gets a signal to stop.
Want to break it? Add friction. Move the app off your home screen, and the habit gets weaker.
But first, follow. This is the one scroll worth stopping for.
