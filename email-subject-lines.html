25 email subject lines that get opened

4 min read

Your email is only as good as its subject line — if it isn’t opened, nothing else matters. Here are 25 proven subject lines you can adapt, plus the rules behind them.

Sales & promotions

  • Your 20% off ends tonight
  • We saved you something 👀
  • The sale you asked for is here
  • Last chance: 3 hours left
  • Free shipping, this weekend only

Product launches

  • It’s finally here
  • Meet the newest addition to the family
  • You’re first to see this
  • We made this because you asked
  • New: [product] (and why we love it)

Newsletters

  • 3 things worth your time this week
  • What we learned this month
  • The one tip that changed how we work
  • Quick question for you
  • Behind the scenes at [brand]

Win-back & reminders

  • We miss you (and here’s 15% off)
  • Still thinking about it?
  • You left something in your cart
  • Is this goodbye?
  • A small thank-you for being with us

Outreach & B2B

  • Idea for [company]’s Instagram
  • 15 minutes next week?
  • Quick win for your [specific goal]
  • Saw your post about [topic]
  • [Mutual contact] suggested I reach out

5 rules for subject lines that get opened

  1. Keep it under 45 characters so it isn’t cut off on phones.
  2. Create curiosity or urgency — but never lie. Misleading subjects destroy trust.
  3. Personalize with the reader’s name, company or last purchase when you can.
  4. Use one emoji at most. It can help in promotions; it hurts in B2B.
  5. Test two versions. Most email tools let you A/B test the subject on a small group first.
