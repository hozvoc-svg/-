How to reply to customer reviews (with templates)

5 min read

Every review is a public conversation. Future customers read your replies as carefully as the reviews themselves. Here’s how to answer, with templates you can adapt.

The 4-step reply formula

  1. Thank them by name. It shows a real person read the review.
  2. Mention something specific they wrote, so it doesn’t sound copy-pasted.
  3. Fix or explain — for negative reviews, apologise once and say what you’re doing about it.
  4. Invite them back or offer to continue privately.

Templates for positive reviews

  • “Thank you, Anna! We’re so happy you loved the cinnamon buns — we’ll save you one next time ☕”
  • “Thanks for the kind words, Mark. Our team works hard on fast delivery, and it means a lot that you noticed.”
  • “Five stars from a regular — that’s the best compliment! See you on Saturday?”
  • “We really appreciate you taking the time to write this, Priya. Enjoy your new boots!”

Templates for negative reviews

  • “Hi Tom, I’m sorry your order arrived late — that’s not the experience we want for you. I’ve sent you a direct message with a refund for the delivery cost.”
  • “Thank you for the honest feedback, Lisa. You’re right that the wait was too long on Friday. We’ve added a second barista for weekends.”
  • “I’m sorry the size didn’t fit, Carlos. Returns are free — reply to your order email and we’ll send a new size right away.”
  • “We hear you, and we’re sorry. Could you email us at the address on our website so we can make this right?”

Mistakes to avoid

  • Arguing with the customer in public — even when they’re wrong.
  • Replying weeks later. Aim for 24–48 hours.
  • The same generic reply under every review.
  • Sharing personal details or order numbers publicly.
