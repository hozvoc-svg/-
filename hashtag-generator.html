Questions

How many hashtags should I use?

On Instagram, 3–10 well-chosen hashtags usually work better than 30 random ones. Mix a few popular tags with niche and local ones that describe exactly what you post.

Do hashtags still matter?

Yes, but as a way to describe your content to the platform and to people searching a topic. Relevance matters more than volume.
