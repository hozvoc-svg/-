Four psychology tricks that work almost too well.
Number one. Want someone to like you? Ask them for a small favor. It's called the Ben Franklin effect: helping you makes their brain decide you're worth helping.
Number two. Made a small mistake? Good. Competent people who slip up once seem more likable. That's the Pratfall effect.
Number three. Ask a question, then stay silent. Most people can't stand a pause, so they keep talking and tell you more.
Number four. People remember the peak and the end of an experience. So always finish strong.
