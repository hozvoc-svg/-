## Facts to keep straight
- Monetization in two weeks is not realistic for a new channel; say so plainly and plan for months.
- Mass-produced, repetitive AI content can be refused for monetization; each video needs its own story or scene set.
- Long ambience videos (1–3 h) earn far more watch hours than 20-minute ones.
