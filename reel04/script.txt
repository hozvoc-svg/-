Why does your brain replay that embarrassing moment at 3 AM?
When you're lying in bed, there are no distractions. So your mind starts wandering, and it goes straight to the past.
Your brain is wired to remember social mistakes. For our ancestors, being rejected by the group was dangerous.
But here's the good news. It's called the spotlight effect. People notice your mistakes way less than you think. That moment you remember? They forgot it years ago.
