35 Instagram caption ideas for small businesses

6 min read

Stuck on what to write under your next post? Here are 35 ready-to-use Instagram captions for small businesses, grouped by industry. Copy them, tweak a word or two, and post.

Each one follows a simple rule that works on Instagram: a short, scroll-stopping first line, and a clear reason to comment, save or visit.

Coffee shops & cafés

  • Monday called. We answered with espresso. ☕
  • Your sign to take a 10-minute break is right here.
  • Fresh pastries, fresh start. Come say hi before 10am 🥐
  • Rain outside, cinnamon latte inside. Fair trade.
  • Tag the friend who owes you a coffee 👇

Beauty salons

  • New week, new nails. Who’s booking? 💅
  • Before ➡️ after. Swipe to see the glow-up.
  • Self-care isn’t selfish. Two spots left this Friday.
  • Your hair, but on its best day. Every day.
  • Booked, blessed and blow-dried. Link in bio to reserve.

Clothing & fashion stores

  • Restocked and ready to sell out (again). 🛍️
  • One jacket, three looks. Which one is you?
  • Weekend plans: this outfit. That’s it, that’s the plan.
  • Comfort that doesn’t look like comfort.
  • Only 12 left in this color. Just saying 👀

Fitness & gyms

  • You don’t have to be great to start. You have to start to be great.
  • Monday motivation, delivered at 7am sharp. See you there 💪
  • Progress, not perfection. Show us your first-week win 👇
  • Free trial class this Saturday. Bring a friend, bring water.
  • Sore today, strong tomorrow.

Restaurants & food

  • Made from scratch. Eaten in seconds. 🍝
  • Your table is waiting (and so is dessert).
  • Date night, sorted. Reserve via the link in bio.
  • New on the menu — and already a staff favorite.
  • Comment your go-to order and we’ll guess your personality 😄

Online shops & e-commerce

  • Free shipping ends at midnight. You know what to do. 🛒
  • Customer favorite for a reason: 4.9★ and counting.
  • Gift idea, solved. Wrapped and shipped in 24 hours.
  • Small business, big love. Every order is packed by hand. 💛
  • Back in stock — but not for long.

Any business

  • Behind the scenes of a very ordinary (very busy) Tuesday.
  • Meet the team that makes it happen 👋
  • You asked, we listened. Here’s what’s new.
  • Thank you for 1,000 followers! Giveaway details below 🎉
  • Save this post for later — you’ll thank us.

How to write your own captions that work

  1. Lead with the hook. Only the first line shows before “more”. Make it a question, a bold claim or a relatable feeling.
  2. Add one concrete detail. A time, a price, a flavor, a number. Specific beats generic.
  3. Ask for one action. Comment, save, tag a friend or tap the link — pick one, not all four.
  4. Keep hashtags at the end. 3–8 relevant hashtags are plenty.
