If your life hit rock bottom, you'd fix it fast. But if it's just "fine"? You can stay there for years.
Psychologists call this the region-beta paradox.
Big pain forces you to act. Mild pain is easy to ignore.
That's why the okay job, the okay relationship, the okay habits last the longest.
"Fine" is not a reason to stay. Send this to someone who's stuck at "fine."
