How to write an Etsy listing that sells

5 min read

On Etsy, your listing has two readers: the search engine and the shopper. A great listing works for both. Here’s a template that does.

1. The title: what it is, for whom, what makes it special

Lead with the words shoppers actually type, then add details. Keep it readable — Etsy titles can be up to 140 characters, but the first few words matter most.

Soy Candle — Lavender & Cedar, Hand-Poured, 40-Hour Burn, Gift for Her

2. The first two lines

Only the beginning of your description shows before “Read more”. Use it for the main benefit and the feeling, not for shipping policies.

3. Benefit bullets

  • What it’s made of (and why that’s better)
  • Size, weight and what’s included
  • How long it lasts or how to care for it
  • Who it’s perfect for (gift ideas sell)
  • Personalisation options, if any

4. Tags

Etsy allows 13 tags. Use all of them, mix broad and specific phrases (“lavender candle”, “gift for mom”, “cozy home decor”), and don’t repeat your title word for word.

[Main benefit in one line]
[1–2 sentences: who it’s for and how it feels]
• Made from …

• Perfect for …
