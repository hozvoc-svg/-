FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know SQL?

No. Ask questions in plain language and Lumen generates and runs the queries for you. Power users can still view and edit the SQL.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, we are SOC 2 Type II certified, and you control exactly which tables Lumen can read.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. There are no long-term contracts on the Starter and Pro plans — cancel from your settings with one click.

Which integrations are supported?

Over 150, including Postgres, MySQL, BigQuery, Snowflake, Stripe, HubSpot, Segment and Google Analytics.
