FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I use the content commercially?

Yes. You can use everything you generate in ads, online stores, social media and client work.

What is a credit?

One generation uses one credit. Free accounts get 10 credits every month; paid plans refill on each billing date. If a generation fails, the credit is returned automatically.

Is my content private?

Your prompts and results are visible only to you. Muse uses Anthropic's Claude API, and your content is not used to train AI models.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no contracts — cancel from “Manage billing” in your account and keep access until the end of the paid period.

Does Muse create images or videos?

Not yet. Muse focuses on writing great text — including scripts for your videos and listings for your online store.
