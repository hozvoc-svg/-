FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I use the content commercially?

Yes. On paid plans you own everything you generate and can use it in ads, products and client work.

What is a credit?

One text generation costs 1 credit, one image costs 4 credits and one video clip costs 20 credits. Unused credits roll over for one month.

Is my data used to train models?

No. Your prompts, uploads and brand kits stay private and are never used for training.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. No contracts — cancel from your settings with one click and keep access until the end of the billing period.
