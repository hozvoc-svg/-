Three phrases manipulators use, and how to respond.
Number one. "You're overreacting." It makes you doubt your own feelings. Say: "My feelings are valid, even if you don't share them."
Number two. "After everything I've done for you." It turns kindness into debt. Say: "I appreciate it, but that doesn't mean I owe you this."
Number three. "If you really loved me, you would." It's guilt dressed up as love. Say: "I do care. And my answer is still no."
