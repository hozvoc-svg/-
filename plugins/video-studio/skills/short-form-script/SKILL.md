# Short-form script & storyboard / Сценарій і розкадровка

## Before writing

Confirm or infer: platform + aspect (9:16 / 16:9 / 1:1), length, genre (see `genre-playbooks`), visual style (see `video-styles`), language, voice (on-camera / voiceover / no voice), the single **payoff**. If something is missing, pick a sensible default and state it — don't stall.

## Structure (for 30–60 s; scale proportionally)

| Time | Beat | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | **Hook** | Use the `video-hooks` skill. Three layers. |
| 3–8 s | **Context / stakes** | Why this matters, in one line. Re-hook with a visual change. |
| 8–40 s | **Body** — 3 escalating beats | Each beat ≤ 8 s, each ends with a mini open loop ("але найцікавіше — далі"). |
| 40–52 s | **Payoff** | Deliver exactly what the hook promised. Biggest visual moment. |
| 52–60 s | **Loop / CTA** | Last line flows back into the first frame, or one soft CTA. Never "підпишись" as the hook. |

### Retention rules
- Visual change every **1.5–3 s** (cut, zoom, angle, text pop, B-roll). In calm genres (ASMR, cinematic) every 3–5 s is fine.
- One idea per shot. Captions on every spoken word for social platforms.
- Kill all filler words; spoken pace ~150–170 wpm for Shorts.
- Put a "re-hook" around 25–30% and 60% of runtime (new question, twist, number reveal).
- Keep text inside the safe zone: avoid the top 12% and bottom 20% of a 9:16 frame (UI overlays).

## Output format — shot list table

| # | Time | Shot / camera | Visual (AI prompt in English if generated) | Voice / dialogue | On-screen text | SFX / music | Transition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0–1.4 | ECU, push-in | … | "…" | … | whoosh + bass hit | hard cut |

After the table give:
1. **Voiceover script** as clean text (for ElevenLabs), with `...` for pauses and CAPS for stress sparingly.
2. **Asset list**: which shots to generate (AI video / image), film, or take from stock.
3. **Music brief**: genre, BPM, mood, where the drop lands (match drop to the payoff).
4. **Title + thumbnail/cover idea** (3 options) and caption with 3–5 hashtags.

## Long-form (YouTube 8–20 min) differences
- Hook = first 30 s: cold open of the best moment + promise + "why trust me".
- Chapters every 1.5–3 min, each opening with its own mini-hook.
- Pattern interrupt (B-roll, graphic, music change) every 20–40 s.
