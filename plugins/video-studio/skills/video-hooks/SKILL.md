# Video Hooks / Хуки

A hook is three layers at once. Always write all three, never only the spoken line:

| Layer | What | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Visual** (кадр) | What the viewer sees in frame 1 | Motion or a strange/beautiful image from frame 0. No logo, no black, no slow fade. |
| **Audio** (звук) | First spoken words, SFX or music hit | Sound starts at 0.0 s. A whoosh, impact, voice or beat drop. |
| **Text** (текст на екрані) | ≤ 7 words, big, centre-safe | Says something *different* from the voice — adds a second reason to stay. |

## Workflow

1. Ask (or infer) four things: **platform**, **genre**, **audience**, **the payoff** (what the viewer gets by the end). The hook must promise that payoff — a hook that promises something the video doesn't deliver kills retention later.
2. Generate **10 hooks** across at least 5 different formulas below.
3. Score each with the checklist, show the **top 3** as full three-layer specs.
4. For the winner, give 2 A/B variants (change one layer only).

## Formulas (pick across types)

1. **Curiosity gap** — "Ніхто не помітив, що в цьому кадрі…" / "This looks normal until…"
2. **Result first** — show the final transformation, then "ось як" / "here's how".
3. **Pattern interrupt** — unexpected visual: object falls into frame, hard zoom, freeze, glitch, colour flip.
4. **Direct callout** — "Якщо ти монтуєш у CapCut — зупинись." Names the viewer.
5. **Contrarian** — "Усі радять X. Це помилка."
6. **Stakes / countdown** — "У тебе 3 секунди, щоб…", ticking timer on screen.
7. **In medias res** — start in the middle of action / the climax, then rewind ("12 годин тому…").
8. **Question with a twist** — a question the viewer *thinks* they know the answer to.
9. **List promise** — "3 стилі, які ще ніхто не використовує" (number visible on screen).
10. **Sensory / ASMR** — close macro shot + crisp sound, no words for 1 s.
11. **Story cold open** — one line of dialogue mid-conflict ("Не відкривай ці двері.").
12. **Visual loop setup** — first frame matches the last frame, so the video loops seamlessly.

## Genre cheat sheet

| Genre | Best formulas | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Horror / mystery | 7, 11, 1 | Dark frame with one detail wrong; low drone + silence cut. |
| Comedy | 3, 11, 8 | Start on the funniest face/reaction; never explain the joke in the hook. |
| Education / how-to | 2, 9, 5 | Show the end result in frame 1. |
| Product / ads | 2, 4, 6 | Problem visible in < 1 s; product by second 2. |
| Story / drama | 7, 11 | Mid-conflict line, character close-up. |
| Motivation | 5, 4 | Bold claim + intense music hit. |
| Satisfying / ASMR / process | 10, 12 | No talking; perfect loop. |
| Anime / animation | 3, 7, 11 | Impact frame, speed lines, smear on frame 1. |
| Cinematic / travel | 3, 10 | Drone push-in or whip-pan; beat drop on cut 1. |

## Scoring checklist (0–2 each, keep ≥ 9/12)

- Movement or change on screen in the first 0.5 s
- Understandable with sound **off** (text layer carries it)
- Understandable with eyes **off** (audio layer carries it)
- Creates one specific open question
- Promises the real payoff of the video
- ≤ 12 spoken words before the first cut

## Output format

```
HOOK #1 — <formula> — score 11/12
0.0–0.5s  VISUAL: …
0.0–2.0s  VOICE:  "…"
0.3s      TEXT:   "…"
SFX/MUSIC: …
CUT at 1.2s to: …
```

Write voice lines in the language of the video (default: the user's language); write AI-video prompts for the visual in English (see the `video-styles` skill).
