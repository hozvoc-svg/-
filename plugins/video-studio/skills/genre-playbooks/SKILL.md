# Genre playbooks / Жанри

For each genre: **structure → pacing → sound → look → captions → mistakes**. Combine with `video-hooks`, `short-form-script`, `video-styles`.

## Horror / creepypasta
- Structure: normal → one detail wrong → escalation ×3 → reveal cut to black (or cliffhanger for part 2).
- Pacing: slow (3–6 s shots), then very fast at the scare. Silence right before the scare.
- Sound: low drone, room tone, heartbeat, reversed reverb, sudden silence; voice deep and slow.
- Look: cold desaturated, crushed blacks, VHS or found footage, vignette.
- Captions: small, white, typewriter reveal. Mistakes: jump scare in the first 2 s with no setup; music too loud.

## Comedy / skit
- Structure: premise in 1 line → escalate absurdity ×3 → punchline → button (extra small joke).
- Pacing: cut on the laugh, remove every breath; reaction shots 0.5–1 s; zoom-punch on punchline.
- Sound: record-scratch, crickets, bonk, laugh-free; music stops dead for punchlines.
- Look: bright, saturated. Captions: big bold, emoji sparingly. Mistakes: explaining the joke; long setup.

## Education / explainer
- Structure: result first → 3 steps / facts → recap → payoff.
- Pacing: B-roll or graphic every 2–3 s; numbered on-screen labels.
- Sound: light lo-fi/corporate pop under voice at −20 dB; pops on each text reveal.
- Look: clean, isometric 3D or motion graphics, highlight boxes/arrows. Mistakes: intro about yourself before value.

## Product ad / UGC
- Structure (PAS): Problem (visible) → Agitate → Solution (product by 2–3 s) → proof (demo, review) → offer/CTA.
- Pacing: 1–2 s cuts; hands-on demo close-ups. Sound: trending sound or upbeat pop; satisfying product SFX.
- Look: natural phone-shot feel for UGC; macro studio for premium. Mistakes: logo first; claims without demo.

## Storytime / drama
- Structure: in medias res → backstory in 2 lines → rising conflict → twist → resolution/cliffhanger.
- Pacing: follows emotion; hold on faces. Sound: cinematic piano/strings, swell at twist.
- Look: cinematic realism or anime. Captions: word-by-word karaoke. Mistakes: chronological start.

## Motivation
- Structure: bold claim → struggle image → turning point → call to act.
- Pacing: build from slow to fast; cut on beat. Sound: epic hybrid trailer music, deep voice, risers.
- Look: high contrast, golden or bleach bypass, slow motion. Mistakes: generic quotes without a story.

## True crime / mystery / facts
- Structure: the strangest fact first → timeline → evidence beats → open question.
- Pacing: medium, map/photo zooms (Ken Burns), red-string graphics. Sound: tense pulse, typewriter SFX.
- Look: noir, archival grain, document close-ups. Mistakes: unverified claims — always flag uncertainty.

## Satisfying / ASMR / process
- Structure: start mid-process → 3 stages → perfect finish → seamless loop.
- Pacing: 3–5 s shots, no talking; speed-ramp boring parts. Sound: crisp foley, no music or very soft.
- Look: macro, top-down, clean background. Mistakes: music covering the sounds.

## Travel / cinematic
- Structure: wow shot → journey montage on beat → human moment → closing wide shot.
- Pacing: cut on beat, speed ramps, whip-pan transitions, match cuts. Sound: one strong track, ambient layers.
- Look: teal-orange or warm golden, drone + FPV. Mistakes: too many similar landscape shots.

## Gaming
- Structure: best clip first → context → 2–3 highlights escalating → fail/win payoff.
- Pacing: fast, zoom-punch, freeze frames with text. Sound: meme SFX, bass boosts on kills. Mistakes: long menus/loading.

## Music video / lyric video
- Cut on beat and on lyric stresses; kinetic typography for lyrics; recurring visual motif; colour shift at the chorus.

## Kids
- Bright saturated stylized 3D or watercolor; slow clear voice; repetition; gentle SFX; no scary cuts; 2–4 s shots.

## News / quick facts
- Number on screen in frame 1; source labels; neutral music; 1 fact per 4–6 s; correct, cited, dated info.
