You miss them all day. Then they finally call, and you start a fight. Why?
You saved up stories for them, but they sound distracted. And your brain hears: I don't matter.
Psychologists call it protest behavior. The anger isn't really anger. It's a bid for connection.
So instead of "You never listen," try: "I missed you. Can I have five minutes?"
Send this to the person you miss the most.
